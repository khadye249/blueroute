Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a one way cab, outstation taxi or airport transfer with BlueRoute Cabs.

Booking

Choose One Way, Round Trip or Airport in the booking form, enter pickup and drop, pick your date, time and vehicle, and add your mobile number. You instantly see a fare estimate and our team confirms your booking on call or WhatsApp within minutes. You can also call or WhatsApp us 24x7.

Yes. For Mumbai and Pune city pickups we usually arrange a cab within 60-90 minutes, subject to availability. Call or WhatsApp us for the fastest confirmation.

Pricing

One way fares are charged per kilometre for the single leg only, with a minimum of 130 km. Driver allowance is included. Tolls, parking and state entry taxes are extra at actuals and are confirmed before you travel.

A one way cab is priced for a single drop with no return charge. A round trip is billed on the greater of the actual distance or 250 km per day, plus driver allowance per night. If you only need to travel in one direction, one way is almost always cheaper.

No. The fare estimate lists the distance, rate per km and driver allowance. Tolls and parking are charged at actuals against receipts. We confirm the final quote before you travel.

Payments

UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and cash. You can pay the driver at the end of the trip or request a secure payment link. Corporate customers get monthly invoicing.

Cancel free up to 2 hours before pickup. For late cancellations a small cancellation fee may apply. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

Airport

Yes. Share your flight number and we track the arrival. Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting after landing, and your driver meets you at arrivals with a name board.

Yes. We provide fixed-fare pickups and drops at Mumbai Airport (T1 and T2), Navi Mumbai Airport and Pune Airport, plus transfers between the two cities.

Safety

Every chauffeur completes ID and police verification, a driving licence check and a safety briefing. Cars are sanitised before trips and trips can be shared live with family.

All vehicles are commercial-permit, insured, GPS-enabled and serviced on schedule. Child seats are available on request.

Corporate

Yes - monthly invoicing with GST, dedicated chauffeurs, employee airport transfers, event shuttles and travel desks. Fill the corporate enquiry form for a tailored rate card.

Trips

Yes. Hire a chauffeur driven car for local sightseeing, multi-day tours and pilgrimage circuits like Ashtavinayak, Shirdi and Nashik. See our tourist packages.

Absolutely. Choose an Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta or Tempo Traveller for families and luggage-heavy trips. Each vehicle card lists seating and luggage capacity.
