1. Booking and confirmation

A booking request submitted on this website or by phone/WhatsApp is confirmed only when our team confirms it to you with vehicle and driver details. Fare estimates shown online are indicative until confirmed.

2. Fares and charges

  • One way trips are billed per kilometre for the single leg with a minimum of 130 km. Driver allowance is included.
  • Round trips are billed on the greater of actual kilometres or 250 km per day, plus driver allowance for each overnight stay.
  • Airport transfers are billed at a base fare covering the first 15 km, plus the airport per-km rate beyond that. Up to 60 minutes of waiting after flight landing is free.
  • Extras: tolls, parking, state entry taxes, inter-state permits, and entry/monument fees are payable at actuals. GST is applicable as per law.
  • Waiting beyond free limits, additional stops or route changes may attract additional charges, which will be communicated by the driver or travel desk.

3. Payment

Payment can be made by UPI, card, net banking or cash as agreed at booking. Corporate customers may be invoiced monthly under a separate agreement.

4. Cancellation and refunds

  • Cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup.
  • Cancellations after that, or no-shows, may incur a cancellation fee to cover driver dispatch costs.
  • If we cancel or cannot provide a vehicle, you will receive a full refund of any amount paid.
