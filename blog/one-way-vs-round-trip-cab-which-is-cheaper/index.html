One Way vs Round Trip Cab: Which Is Cheaper for Outstation Travel?

Fare Guide20 September 20264 min read

Both options get you to your destination. The right one depends on whether you need the car after you arrive. Here is a simple way to decide.

How one way pricing works

A one way outstation taxi is billed for the distance from pickup to drop, with a minimum of 130 km. You are not charged for the driver’s return trip. Driver allowance is included.

How round trip pricing works

A round trip is billed on the greater of the actual kilometres or 250 km per day, plus a driver allowance for each night. You keep the same car and driver for the entire trip.

Worked example: Mumbai to Pune (150 km, sedan)

  • One way: 150 km × ₹13 = about ₹1,950
  • Same-day round trip: 300 km × ₹11 = about ₹3,300

If you only need to travel one way, you save roughly ₹1,350 by booking one way.

A simple rule of thumb

  • Choose one way if you are going to a destination and returning by train, flight or another cab.
  • Choose round trip if you need local sightseeing, multiple stops or the same driver for the return leg.
  • Staying 3+ days? Compare a round trip with local sightseeing packages at the destination.

Tips to save more on outstation cabs

  • Book early for weekends and holidays when demand rises.
  • Pick the right size – a sedan is cheaper than an SUV for 3 passengers.
  • Travel off-peak when possible to avoid additional waiting time.
